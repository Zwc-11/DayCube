# Consent workflow — not implemented yet

This development build is intended for fictional/test data. It does not yet provide the consent,
face blur or deletion controls required for recording real participants.

Before a volunteer study or personal capture release, the project needs a reviewed consent form
that names every captured source, local and outbound storage, who can access it, retention,
withdrawal/deletion procedure, and the handling of bystanders. Do not present this placeholder
as a signed or approved consent form. The sample fixture contains no real recordings.
