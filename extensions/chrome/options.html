Settings

The text in vault/.token. It stays in this browser's extension storage and is never given to web pages.

One per line. Subdomains are included: mybank.com also covers online.mybank.com.

When your eyes stay on one passage

A dwell is fixation time of at least 8 seconds and twice the time the passage takes to read. Every dwell is recorded whichever you choose.

The bar is added to the page itself, so the site's own scripts can see it and which passage it marks.

A small blue marker beside the line the eye tracker decodes, updated every second; fainter when less sure. Like the bar, it is added to the page, so the site's own scripts can see it.

What is recorded

  • Each page you open: its address, title and how you got there (a link, typed, back, reload).
  • Which tab is in front, and when the browser loses focus or you are away.
  • Searches on Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, YouTube, GitHub, Wikipedia and others.
  • Clicks on links and buttons, text you select or copy, and video or audio you play or pause.
  • The text visible on screen and where it is, line by line, so gaze can be matched to paragraphs and lines.

What is not

  • Private windows and excluded domains (links to them are recorded without their address).
  • The text, clicks, selections and media of a page while it or a frame in it shows a password field. Its address and title are still recorded.
  • Anything you type, in fields, editable areas or code editors.
  • Anything while paused. Click the toolbar button or press Alt+Shift+D to pause or resume; this also stops the eye tracker and releases the webcam, even when browser recording is off.
